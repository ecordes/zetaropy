Howl.

Cautious, but reliably in the middle of whatever you were doing.

Howl had a gift for choosing the exact spot you most needed, and settling into it completely.

A jigsaw puzzle half finished on the table. A book left open. The one warm patch of the rug. Wherever the day's activity was happening, that is where he lay down, at full length, with the serene confidence of a cat who has never been asked to move and does not expect to be.

Pale cream, extravagantly fluffy, with wide green eyes and a ruff that took up more room than he did. Gentle company, endlessly patient, and the softest thing in any room he was in.
